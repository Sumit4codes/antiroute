🍎 Apple Shortcuts (iPhone Siri Voice)

Ask Gemini questions hands-free directly using Siri on your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on iOS.
  2. Tap + to create a new shortcut named "Ask Gemini".
  3. Add action: Dictate Text (or Ask for Input).
  4. Add action: Get Contents of URL:
    • URL: http://10.166.250.215:8317/v1/chat/completions
    • Method: POST
    • Headers: Authorization: Bearer sk-antigravity-claude, Content-Type: application/json
    • Request Body (JSON): {"model":"gemini-3.8-flash-high", "messages":[{"role":"user","content":"(Dictated Text)"}]}
  5. Add action: Get Dictionary Value: choices.1.message.content.
  6. Add action: Speak Text (to hear Gemini reply).

Now say: "Hey Siri, Ask Gemini" from anywhere on your phone!
